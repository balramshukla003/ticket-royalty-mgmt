# Book Status & Production Knowledge Base

## Production stages (in order)
1. Manuscript Received
2. Editing (if opted)
3. Cover Design
4. Typesetting
5. Proofreading
6. ISBN Assignment
7. Printing
8. Distribution Setup
9. Published & Live

- Authors are updated at each stage via email.
- Delays typically happen at Cover Design (waiting for author approval) and Proofreading (revision rounds).

## Response guidance
- Check the actual production status and mention it specifically.
- If delayed, be honest about the reason (e.g., waiting on author approval for proofs).
- Provide a specific updated timeline — never give vague reassurances.
- Frame delays collaboratively, not as the author's fault even if it is.
- If at Cover Design: mention that a proof will be sent for approval within X business days.
- If at Typesetting: mention that formatted pages will be ready for review within X business days.

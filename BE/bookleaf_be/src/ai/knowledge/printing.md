# Printing & Quality Knowledge Base

- In-house printing handles most orders. Overflow or specific format requirements go to Repro India or Epitome Books.
- Standard print turnaround: 5–7 business days from order confirmation.
- If an author reports a quality issue (misprints, binding defects, color inconsistency), BookLeaf arranges a FREE reprint after verification.
- The author may need to share photos of the defective copy for verification.
- Print partners: In-House (Delhi), Repro India, Epitome Books.

## Response guidance
- Apologise sincerely for quality defects — this is BookLeaf's responsibility.
- Ask the author to share photos of the defective copies.
- Confirm that BookLeaf will arrange a free reprint once verified.
- Provide a timeline of 5–7 business days for the reprint after verification.
- Never be defensive about print quality issues.

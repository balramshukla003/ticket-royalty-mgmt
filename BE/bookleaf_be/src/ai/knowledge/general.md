# General Inquiry Knowledge Base

## About BookLeaf Publishing
- BookLeaf Publishing is a self-publishing company operating in India and the US.
- Publishing packages: Standard Free (no upfront cost) and Bestseller Breakthrough (premium, paid, with marketing and distribution add-ons).
- Services: cover design, typesetting, ISBN assignment, printing, distribution, and royalty management.
- In-house printing facility and warehouse located in Delhi.
- Print partners: Repro India and Epitome Books.

## Common general queries
- Metadata updates (book description, author bio): submit via dashboard or email support@bookleaf.com. Reflects in 3–5 business days.
- Cover updates: submit a request via email. Treated as a production task.
- Publishing package upgrades: contact the BookLeaf team.

## Response guidance
- Be helpful and specific.
- Always end with a clear next step for the author.
- Tone: professional, warm, like a knowledgeable colleague — not a corporate FAQ.

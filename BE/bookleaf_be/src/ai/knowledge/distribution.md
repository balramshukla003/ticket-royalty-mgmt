# Distribution & Availability Knowledge Base

- Books are listed on: Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, and the BookLeaf Store.
- New listings typically go live within 7–10 business days after publication is complete.
- If a book shows as "Currently Unavailable" on a platform, it usually indicates a stock sync issue.
- BookLeaf's team can trigger a re-sync within 24–48 hours.

## Response guidance
- Explain that "Currently Unavailable" is typically a stock sync issue, not a permanent problem.
- Confirm that you are triggering a re-sync with the distribution team.
- Set expectation: 24–48 hours for the book to go live again.
- If the book was never listed, confirm the 7–10 business day timeline from publication completion.

# Royalty & Payments Knowledge Base

- BookLeaf follows an 80/20 royalty split: 80% of net profit goes to the author, 20% to BookLeaf.
- Net profit = MRP minus printing cost, platform commission (Amazon/Flipkart), and shipping charges.
- Royalties are calculated quarterly and paid within 45 days of the quarter ending.
  - Q1 (Jan–Mar): paid by May 15
  - Q2 (Apr–Jun): paid by Aug 15
  - Q3 (Jul–Sep): paid by Nov 15
  - Q4 (Oct–Dec): paid by Feb 15
- Minimum payout threshold: ₹1,000. If accumulated royalties are below this, they roll over to the next quarter.
- Payouts are made via bank transfer to the account linked in the author's dashboard.
- Authors can view a detailed royalty breakdown in their dashboard showing sales figures per platform.
- If an author has not linked their bank details, payouts cannot be processed.

## Response guidance
- Always acknowledge the frustration before explaining the system.
- Provide the specific next payout date based on the current quarter.
- If the amount seems low, explain the net profit calculation clearly — MRP is not the royalty base.
- If payment is genuinely overdue (past 45 days from quarter end), escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline.
- If royalty_pending is below ₹1,000, explain the rollover policy — do NOT treat this as overdue.
- If bank_details_linked is false, ask the author to link their bank account first.
